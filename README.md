# 🧲 Inside the Magnet: Physics, MRI Hardware, and Patient Safety 

An interactive, open-source educational guide dedicated to the static magnetic field in Magnetic Resonance Imaging, focusing on hardware design, safety protocols, and physiological effects.

## 📖 Content Overview
* **Magnetization:** Protons, spin alignment, and the physics of the static $B_0$ field.
* **Hardware & Safety:** Superconducting magnets, Ampère's Law, fringe fields, and sustainable low-helium technology (DryCool).
* **Image Quality:** How scaling the $B_0$ field strength impacts Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR), tissue contrast, and susceptibility.
* **Physiological Effects:** How the magnetic environment interacts with the human body, including sensory effects, tissue heating (SAR), and nerve stimulation (PNS).
