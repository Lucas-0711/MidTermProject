# The Main Magnetic Field ($B_0$)

The cornerstone of any Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) system is the main magnetic field, conventionally denoted as $B_0$ and oriented along the z-axis. This field is responsible for creating the fundamental signal source we measure: the net magnetization.

## Polarization and Signal Strength

The primary purpose of the $B_0$ field is **polarization**. When a patient is placed inside the scanner, their nuclear spins (primarily hydrogen protons in water and fat) align preferentially with the external magnetic field. 

The relationship is direct and crucial for image quality:
*   **Stronger $B_0$ $\rightarrow$ Greater spin alignment $\rightarrow$ Larger net magnetization ($M_z$) $\rightarrow$ Higher Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR).**
